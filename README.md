
# My Favorite Movie: I Want to Eat Your Pancreas

## About the Movie
**I Want to Eat Your Pancreas** is a Japanese animated movie about a quiet high school boy who discovers that his classmate, Sakura Yamauchi, has a serious pancreatic illness. Despite their different personalities, they develop a special friendship and spend meaningful moments together.

## Main Characters
- **Sakura Yamauchi** – A cheerful girl who wants to enjoy every moment of her life.
- **Haruki Shiga** – A quiet and introverted boy who becomes close to Sakura.

## Why I Like This Movie
I like this movie because it is emotional, meaningful, and teaches us to appreciate life. It shows the importance of friendship, happiness, and spending time with the people who matter to us.

## My Favorite Part
My favorite part is seeing how Sakura and Haruki slowly become close despite their different personalities. Their friendship makes the story very touching and unforgettable.

## My Rating
**10/10** – This movie is heartbreaking but beautiful. It is one of the most memorable movies I have ever watched.
